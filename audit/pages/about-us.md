## Our Journey

2016The Beginning

In January 2016, Akhtar Waquar, along with co-founders Sabah Akhtar, Mohammad Tauhid, and Akhtar Arif, established MYR Network Security Solution in India. The company started with a strong focus on advanced CCTV security solutions, driven by a vision of innovation, excellence, and technological advancement. With a commitment to delivering high-quality IT and security solutions, MYR set out to provide reliable and tailored technology services to businesses worldwide. 

2017Expanding Horizons

Building on our initial success in security solutions, we expanded our service offerings to include a wider range of IT solutions. This year marked the introduction of CRM customization, digital marketing, and network solutions, allowing us to better meet the evolving needs of our clients and broaden our capabilities. 

2018Rebranding and Growth

As our expertise and offerings grew, we rebranded as MYR Advance Solution Pvt. Ltd. to reflect our expanding capabilities and commitment to excellence. This transition included the formation of strategic partnerships and collaborations with industry professionals, further positioning us as a comprehensive IT solutions provider. 

2019Entering New Markets

With a focus on expanding our reach, we ventured into new markets by introducing toll-free number services and strengthening our digital marketing solutions. These advancements enabled us to offer a more complete suite of communication and marketing tools to our clients. 

2020Adapting to Change

Despite global challenges, we remained resilient, adapting our services to support businesses in an increasingly digital-first world. Through continuous innovation and adaptability, we provided reliable IT solutions while maintaining our high standards of client satisfaction. 

2021Enhancing Client Solutions

This year, we focused on optimizing service delivery and integrating the latest technologies into our solutions. We strengthened our network infrastructure and introduced new cybersecurity measures, ensuring that our clients’ systems remained secure, efficient, and future-ready. 

2022Building Strategic Partnerships

We established key partnerships that enabled us to enhance our IT services and expand into new regions. Our growing client base reflected our commitment to excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction. 

2023Leading with Innovation

With a continued focus on innovation, we introduced advanced cybersecurity measures and expanded our service offerings to stay ahead of technological advancements. Our goal remained to provide cutting-edge IT solutions and superior support, meeting the dynamic needs of our clients. 

2024Commitment to Excellence

As we move forward, we remain committed to excellence, innovation, and customer success. We continue to evolve, offering state-of-the-art technology solutions, strengthening our partnerships, and ensuring businesses and individuals thrive in the digital era.
