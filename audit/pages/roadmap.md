In January, India, Akhtar, supported by a brotherly team of esteemed professionals, founded MYR Network Security Solution. They began their journey with a focus on security solutions, starting with advanced CCTV systems. Driven by a vision of innovation and excellence, they aimed to provide comprehensive IT services and cutting-edge security solutions tailored to the diverse needs of businesses worldwide.

The Beginning 2016

Building on our initial success in security solutions, we expanded our service offerings to include a range of IT solutions. This year marked the introduction of CRM customization, digital marketing, and network solutions, broadening our capabilities to meet the evolving needs of our clients.

Expanding Horizons 2017

we rebranded as MYR Advance Solution Pvt. Ltd. The transition reflected our growing capabilities and commitment to excellence. This rebranding came with the formation of strategic partnerships and collaboration with a team of esteemed professionals, enhancing our service offerings and positioning us as a comprehensive IT solutions provider.

Rebranding and Growth 2018

Our focus shifted to entering new markets and expanding our reach. We launched toll-free number services and advanced our digital marketing efforts, ensuring that our clients had access to a complete suite of communication and marketing tools.

Entering New Markets 2019

Despite global challenges, we remained resilient, adapting our services to support businesses through an increasingly digital landscape. We continued to innovate, providing reliable IT solutions and maintaining a high standard of client satisfaction.

Adapting to Change 2020

We focused on refining our service delivery and optimizing our offerings. Leveraging the latest technologies, we improved our network solutions and introduced new cybersecurity measures to keep our clients' systems secure and efficient.

Enhancing Client Solutions 2021

This year was marked by the establishment of key partnerships, enabling us to enhance our IT services and extend our reach into new regions. Our growing client base reflected our commitment to excellence and innovation.

Building Strategic Partnerships 2022

We continued to lead with innovative solutions, introducing advanced cybersecurity measures and expanding our service offerings. Our focus remained on providing cutting-edge technology and superior support to meet the dynamic needs of our clients.
