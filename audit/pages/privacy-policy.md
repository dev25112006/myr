**Privacy Policy for MYR ADVANCE SOLUTION PVT. LTD.**

**Last Updated: 10-04-2024**

MYR ADVANCE SOLUTION PVT. LTD. (“we”, “our”, or “us”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website **myradvancesolution.com** , services, and products, including IT products such as CCTV, routers, audio AirPods, smartwatches, and more.

### 1\. **Information We Collect**

We collect personal and non-personal information for various purposes, which may include:

  * **Personal Information** : Name, email address, phone number, address, payment details, and other information provided by you when placing an order, submitting inquiries, or interacting with our services.

  * **Non-Personal Information** : IP addresses, browser types, browsing history, and website usage data collected through cookies and other tracking technologies.

### 2\. **How We Use Your Information**

We use your data for purposes including but not limited to:

  * **Service Provision** : To process and deliver the services or products you purchase, including website development, CRM solutions, digital marketing services, and IT products like CCTV and routers.

  * **Customer Support** : To respond to your inquiries, provide technical support, and assist with product or service issues.

  * **Order Fulfillment** : To process payments, arrange product shipments, and manage orders for IT products (e.g., CCTV systems, routers, smartwatches).

  * **Marketing & Communication**: To send marketing communications about our products, special offers, and updates, if you have consented to receive such communications.

  * **Website Improvement** : To analyze website performance and enhance user experience based on non-personal data collected via cookies.

### 3\. **Cookies and Tracking Technologies**

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies help us remember your preferences, analyze website traffic, and provide a personalized experience. You can manage your cookie preferences via your browser settings.

### 4\. **Data Sharing and Disclosure**

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  * **Service Providers** : We may share your data with third-party service providers who help us operate our website, process payments, or deliver our products and services. These partners are required to maintain the confidentiality of your data.

  * **Legal Compliance** : If required by law, we may disclose your personal information to government authorities or comply with legal obligations.

  * **Business Transactions** : In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of the business operations.

### 5\. **Data Security**

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no online data transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 6\. **Your Rights**

You have certain rights concerning your personal data, including:

  * **Access** : You can request access to the personal information we hold about you.

  * **Correction** : You can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.

  * **Deletion** : You can request that we delete your personal data, subject to any legal obligations that require us to retain it.

  * **Opt-Out** : You can opt out of marketing communications by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our emails or contacting us directly.

### 7\. **Third-Party Links**

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any linked websites.

### 8\. **Children’s Privacy**

Our services and products are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly.

### 9\. **Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will post the updated policy on our website and update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

### 10\. **Contact Us**

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or wish to exercise your rights regarding your personal information, please contact us:

  * **Email** : [contact@myradvancesolution.com](mailto:contact@myradvancesolution.com)
