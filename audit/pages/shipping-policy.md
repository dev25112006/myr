**Shipping Policy for MYR ADVANCE SOLUTION PVT. LTD.**

**Last Updated: 10-04-2-24**

At **MYR ADVANCE SOLUTION PVT. LTD.** , we strive to ensure that your orders are processed, shipped, and delivered in a timely manner. This Shipping Policy outlines the terms and conditions related to the shipment of products, including but not limited to CCTV systems, routers, audio AirPods, smartwatches, and other IT products.

### 1\. **Shipping Destinations**

We currently ship to locations within **India**. International shipping may be available for specific products upon request. Please contact our customer service team for more information on international shipping options and rates.

### 2\. **Processing Time**

  * **Order Processing** : Orders are typically processed within **1-3 business days** after payment is confirmed. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

  * **Delays** : In cases of high order volume or unforeseen circumstances, processing may take longer. We will notify you via email or phone in case of any significant delays.

### 3\. **Shipping Methods and Delivery Time**

We offer various shipping methods to meet your needs. Estimated delivery times depend on your location and the shipping option chosen at checkout:

  * **Standard Shipping** : Delivery within **5-7 business days**.

  * **Express Shipping** : Delivery within **2-3 business days**.

  * **International Shipping** : Delivery times vary based on the destination, but typically range from **7-14 business days**.

Please note that delivery times are estimates and not guaranteed. Actual delivery times may vary due to factors beyond our control, such as weather conditions, courier delays, or remote location challenges.

### 4\. **Shipping Charges**

Shipping charges will be calculated at checkout based on your location, the shipping method selected, and the weight or dimensions of the product(s). Shipping fees are non-refundable unless you receive a damaged or incorrect item (see our **Returns and Refunds Policy** for more information).

### 5\. **Order Tracking**

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and instructions on how to track your package. Please allow up to **24 hours** for the tracking information to update.

### 6\. **Undeliverable Packages**

In the event that a package is returned to us due to an incorrect address or failed delivery attempts, we will contact you to arrange reshipment. Please ensure that your shipping address is accurate and complete when placing an order.

If a package cannot be delivered due to incorrect address information or refusal of the package, you may be responsible for additional shipping fees.

### 7\. **Damaged or Lost Shipments**

If you receive a damaged package, please contact us within **48 hours** of delivery. We will work with the shipping carrier to resolve the issue and, if necessary, arrange for a replacement or refund.

In the case of a lost shipment, please notify us as soon as possible. We will file a claim with the shipping carrier and work towards resolving the issue in a timely manner.

### 8\. **Customs and Import Duties (For International Orders)**

For international orders, customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees applicable in their destination country. These charges are not included in our product prices or shipping costs. Please check with your local customs office for more information on potential charges.

### 9\. **Contact Us**

If you have any questions regarding shipping, tracking, or delivery of your order, please reach out to us:

  * **Email** : contact@myradvancesolution.com
